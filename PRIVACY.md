# Tuned In privacy policy

_Last updated: 27 September 2026_

Tuned In recommends a song that matches the mood of the web page you're reading, or of text you paste into it. This policy explains what the extension does with your data.

## What stays on your device

- **Page text.** When you press Generate, Tuned In reads the text of the current tab. It analyses that text with an AI model that runs on your computer: either the model bundled with the extension, or Chrome's built-in Gemini Nano. The text is never sent anywhere.
- **History and settings.** Your recommendations and the pages they came from (title, address and the text the AI read), plus your settings, are saved in your browser's extension storage. They never leave your device. You can export or delete them at any time in Settings.

## What is sent to other services

To find a song, Tuned In sends:

- a few mood and genre words (for example "melancholic, piano") to **Last.fm**;
- a song title and artist name to **Apple's iTunes Search**;
- requests for album artwork and 30-second previews to **Apple's servers**.

None of these requests include page text, page addresses or anything that identifies you. These services receive your IP address as part of any normal web request, and handle it under their own privacy policies:
- [Last.fm privacy policy](https://www.last.fm/legal/privacy)
- [Apple privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/)

If you click a streaming link, that service's own privacy policy applies.

## What Tuned In does not do

There are no accounts, analytics, tracking or ads. The developer runs no servers and receives none of your data. Your data is never sold or shared for any other purpose.
